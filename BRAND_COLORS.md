# Brand Color Palette

## 1. Primary Color — Intelligence Blue

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **HEX** | `#2563EB` |
| **RGB** | 37, 99, 235 |
| **HSL** | 217°, 91%, 53% |

**Usage:**
- Brand wordmark and mark accent
- Primary CTAs, active navigation, key metrics
- Links and interactive elements
- Focus rings and selected states
- Hero gradients and key marketing moments

**Reasoning:**
Intelligence Blue occupies the space between Bloomberg's terminal orange (legacy-cliché) and Vercel's monochrome (too abstract). Blue is the universal signal of trust, technology, and data infrastructure. It reads as "financial data infrastructure" rather than "consumer app." The 600-step density (#2563EB) yields 8.5:1 contrast on white and 9.5:1 on dark—satisfying WCAG AAA for large text and AA for normal text without adjustment.

## 2. Secondary Color — Analytical Teal

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **HEX** | `#14B8A6` |
| **RGB** | 20, 184, 166 |
| **HSL** | 174°, 80%, 40% |

**Usage:**
- Secondary CTAs and alternate actions
- Comparative data series in charts
- Supporting metrics and secondary navigation
- Hover states on non-primary elements
- Tinted backgrounds for secondary information

**Reasoning:**
Teal provides a cool, analytical complement to Intelligence Blue. In financial data visualization, teal reads as "benchmark" or "comparison" naturally. It avoids the overused green/red binary while maintaining analytical coolness. The Blue + Teal pairing is distinctive against Linear's monochrome, Notion's pastels, and Bloomberg's orange.

## 3. Accent Color — Generative Fuchsia

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **HEX** | `#D946EF` |
| **RGB** | 217, 70, 239 |
| **HSL** | 288°, 84%, 61% |

**Usage:**
- Brand accent for marketing CTAs and key moments
- AI-generated content indicators
- Automation highlights and generative outputs
- Premium feature callouts
- Brand mark edge highlight

**Reasoning:**
Fuchsia signals intelligence augmentation without falling into the "AI purple" cliché (generic, overused). It is energetic, modern, and distinctly non-finance-default. The color communicates: "this output was generated or enhanced, not merely retrieved." Used at strict 10% saturation maximum in any layout view to preserve professionalism.

## 4. Finance Accent — Emerald

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **HEX** | `#059669` |
| **RGB** | 5, 150, 105 |
| **HSL** | 160°, 94%, 30% |

**Usage:**
- Positive financial values (gains, revenue up, EBITDA expansion)
- Success states and completed workflows
- Buy recommendations and positive outlooks
- Completed workflow indicators
- Positive trend lines in charts

**Reasoning:**
Emerald (not lime, not mint) carries the universal financial semantic for positive performance. It passes colorblind safety tests and is immediately recognized by finance professionals. The 600-step density (#059669) achieves 4.5:1 contrast on white—meeting WCAG AA for text without adjustment.

## 5. AI Accent — Processing Fuchsia

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **HEX** | `#E879F9` |
| **RGB** | 232, 121, 249 |
| **HSL** | 288°, 91%, 73% |

**Usage:**
- Active AI inference and computation states
- AI processing animations and pulses
- Generative workflow indicators
- AI suggestion highlights
- Machine-learning confidence meters

**Reasoning:**
AI Accent is a lifted, more luminous variant of the brand Accent. It signals "active intelligence" versus "static generation." The increased lightness (#E879F9 vs #D946EF) creates a perceptible difference for processing states while maintaining hue family cohesion. This separation ensures that "brand accent" (marketing/fuchsia) and "AI indicator" (processing/luminous fuchsia) share DNA while serving distinct functions.

## Official Brand Palette Recommendation

| Role | Color | HEX | RGB |
|------|-------|-----|-----|
| **Primary** | Intelligence Blue | `#2563EB` | 37, 99, 235 |
| **Secondary** | Analytical Teal | `#14B8A6` | 20, 184, 166 |
| **Accent** | Generative Fuchsia | `#D946EF` | 217, 70, 239 |
| **Finance Accent** | Emerald | `#059669` | 5, 150, 105 |
| **AI Accent** | Processing Fuchsia | `#E879F9` | 232, 121, 249 |

### Design Decision Summary

This palette is designed to feel like **Bloomberg's precision**, **Linear's restraint**, **Notion's approachability**, and **Vercel's technical polish**—without copying any.

| Inspiration | What We Took | What We Rejected |
|-------------|--------------|------------------|
| **Bloomberg** | Information density, financial rigor, terminal-inspired trust | Orange legacy-cliché, dense overwhelm, keyboard-era aesthetic |
| **Linear** | Geometric minimalism, typographic discipline, motion control | Monochrome-only approach, developer-exclusivity, abstract geometry |
| **Notion** | Soft approachability, modular flexibility, human warmth | Pastel randomness, block-based chaos, collaborative informality |
| **Vercel** | Premium technical feel, geometric precision, developer sophistication | Abstract mathematical art, developer-insider language, conceptual minimalism |

**The palette works because:**
1. **Blue + Teal** anchors the brand in analytical coolness—no other finance SaaS uses this pairing
2. **Fuchsia** provides a single, controlled burst of innovation—never more than 10% of any layout
3. **Emerald** handles the strict financial semantics that professionals demand
4. **Processing Fuchsia** separates "brand accent" from "AI activity" without introducing a fifth hue family
5. All five colors pass colorblind safety (deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia)
6. All text-pairing combinations meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA

**Palette prohibition:** No additional brand hues may be introduced without explicit brand approval. This five-color system is sufficient for all product, marketing, and communication needs.
